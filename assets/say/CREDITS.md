(`js/terms.js`, Say). Each is a real speaker's recording from Wikimedia Commons, kept under its own licence, which
this lab's licences do not change. Each file here is the MP3 that Commons makes of the original. Words with no
recording, and phrases, are said by the browser's own British or American voice.
