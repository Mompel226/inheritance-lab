# Pronunciation recordings

The recordings the 🔊 UK and 🔊 US buttons play, beside the hard words on the key-word cards and in the glossary
(`js/terms.js`, Say). Each is a real speaker's recording from Wikimedia Commons, kept under its own licence, which
this lab's licences do not change. Each file here is the MP3 that Commons makes of the original, its loudness evened
(the only change). Only clear recordings are kept: the voice at least 40 dB above the background, the background at
most −60 dB, and its "s" and "t" sounds not muffled (Daniel, 5 Nov 2026: "some of the recordings are not very good …
just don't include them"). Words with no clear recording, and phrases, are said by the browser's own British or
American voice.
